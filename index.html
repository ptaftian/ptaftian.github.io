About

I build software, then I make sure it survives contact with real hardware and real users.

I work across the stack: Python and Django on the backend, React on the front, and WordPress when a project calls for it. I'm experienced in web development and in designing the UI/UX that goes with it, so the interface and the system behind it get built together instead of bolted onto each other.

Belt 3D printer for medical insoles

Development, hardware & print quality

A belt-based 3D printer built to manufacture orthopedic insoles. I worked on its development and testing — on the machine itself as much as on its configuration.

  • Mechanical maintenance: extruder disassembly, nozzle replacement, belt adjustment
  • Replaced and configured hardware — Duet control board and Raspberry Pi
  • Configured and troubleshot Klipper/Fluidd: printer settings, filament sensors, macros, pause and resume behaviour
  • Tuned TPU-LW printing parameters to stop filament jams and improve extrusion, layer and belt adhesion
  • Extruder PID tuning and thermal troubleshooting for stable heating
  • Print testing and quality control to improve reliability and consistency

3D body analysis system

3D simulation & prediction

A scanner that measures the body — circumferences, fat, muscle and water percentage, BMI — and returns a fitness score from them.

My part was the application that reconstructs the person's body shape as a 3D model in Blender through bpy. From the scan it predicts how that shape would change with training, and a single scrub bar moves the model through the predicted muscle and fat percentages so the change is something you watch rather than read off a table.
